# Zhongkao Reading Lab

A mobile-first site for reading English passages built from the reader's own 生词, marking new words, and now listening to the same passages away from the screen.

## Reading material

**Collection（合集）**:
Any group of stories published together, with its own progress, and listed in the top bar: a placement set, a review batch or a news edition.
_Avoid_: edition (in general), volume, book

**Placement set（摸底短文）**:
A numbered collection (Placement 01, 02, …) written from a standard word list to find which words the reader does not yet know; a new word list (a higher school level, CET-4, …) gets a new placement set.
_Avoid_: screening passages, 筛选短文

**Review batch（生词复现）**:
A collection written from the reader's own 生词 so those words come back; one batch per round of writing, named by its date (Review 11-24). The first four, from before batches were dated, are Review 01–04.
_Avoid_: reading set, 套, recap set

**News edition（新闻）**:
A numbered collection of real news retold for the reader (News 01, …).
_Avoid_: news set, daily news

**Story（篇）**:
One passage inside a collection: a title, paragraphs, and optional illustration.
_Avoid_: article, lesson, chapter

**Sentence pair（句对）**:
One English sentence and its Chinese rendering; the smallest unit a story is made of, and the unit listening moves by.
_Avoid_: line, segment

## Word meanings

**Word list（中考词表）**:
The standard list of about 1,900 zhongkao words, with their forms and Chinese meanings, that placement sets are written from and that every story is checked against.
_Avoid_: dictionary, vocabulary list

**Base dictionary（基础词典）**:
A general English–Chinese dictionary of everyday words, used only so that any word the reader taps has a basic meaning when neither the collection nor the word list gives one.
_Avoid_: fallback dictionary, full dictionary

**Proper noun（专有名词）**:
A capitalised word that no word source explains, such as a name or a place; it is shown as 专有名词 instead of a dictionary meaning.

## Listening

**Listening（听读）**:
Going through a story by ear, typically with the screen off; available in the app only.
_Avoid_: playback, audio mode, read-aloud

**Interleaved narration（交替朗读）**:
Listening where each English sentence is followed by its Chinese rendering.
_Avoid_: bilingual mode, alternating mode

**English-only narration（纯英朗读）**:
Listening to the English sentences alone, for ear training.
_Avoid_: English mode, listening practice

**Track（音轨）**:
A story's recorded English or Chinese voice, one per language, with the time each sentence starts and ends; both narrations are assembled from the same two tracks.
_Avoid_: audio file, recording

**Run（连播）**:
Listening that continues from the chosen story to the end of its collection, then stops.
_Avoid_: playlist, queue

**Sleep timer（睡眠定时）**:
A limit that ends listening after the current story or a set number of minutes, fading out and always stopping at the end of a sentence.

**Double read（两遍）**:
An option that speaks each English sentence twice before moving on.
_Avoid_: repeat, loop

**The app（App）**:
The iPhone app that shows this site and adds listening; everything the site does works the same inside it.
_Avoid_: client, native app, wrapper

## Publishing

**Content agent（内容 agent）**:
An agent that only produces material for the site and never changes its code: the writer agent or the illustrator agent.

**Writer agent（写文 agent）**:
The content agent that writes each review batch from the reader's 生词, including a picture description for every story, and fixes its own material when the content check rejects it.

**Illustrator agent（配图 agent）**:
The content agent (Codex) that draws a picture for every story that has a description but no picture yet, and uploads nothing else.

**Content check（内容检查）**:
The automatic check every piece of new material must pass; it says in plain words which file and field to fix.
