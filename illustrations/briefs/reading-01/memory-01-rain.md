### 第 1 段

1. My cousin and I wanted to see how much rain fell in our garden.
   我和表亲想看看自家花园到底下了多少雨。

2. We had no proper equipment, so we put one cup by the fence and taped a ruler to it.
   我们没有合适的设备，就在围栏边放了一个杯子，再贴上一把尺子。

3. At supper, the rain began with a bang of thunder.
   晚饭时，雷声轰地一响，雨下了起来。

4. An hour later, the cup was full.
   一小时后，杯子满了。

5. A second cup of the same size was almost empty.
   另一个同样大小的杯子却几乎是空的。

6. We had left it beneath a tree.
   我们把它放在树底下了。

### 第 2 段

1. My cousin said the difference seemed too big and asked for an explanation.
   表亲觉得差得太多，问我这是怎么回事。

2. I looked up and realised that the thick layer of leaves had kept most of the rain out.
   我抬头一看，才意识到密密的叶子挡住了大部分雨水。

3. We made a note on our chart and put the cups in separate spots out in the open.
   我们在记录表上记了一笔，把杯子分开放在空旷处。

4. The next shower gave us almost the same reading in both.
   下一场雨过后，两边的读数就差不多了。
