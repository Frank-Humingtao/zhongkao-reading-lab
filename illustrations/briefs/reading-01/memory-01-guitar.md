### 第 1 段

1. In the park, a boy tried to play a tune on an old biscuit tin.
   公园里，一个男孩试着用旧饼干铁盒弹一段曲调。

2. He had found some string on a shelf in the equipment cupboard at home and made his own guitar.
   他在家里器材柜的搁板上找了些细绳，自己动手做了把吉他。

3. When I sat down, one of his three strings slipped loose.
   我坐下时，他的三根弦里有一根松脱了。

4. He tucked it through a hole in the tin, but the tin fell with a bang.
   他把弦塞进铁盒上的一个孔里，铁盒却砰的一声掉到了地上。

### 第 2 段

1. I helped him mend it with a thick piece of string from my bag.
   我从包里拿出一根粗绳，帮他修好了。

2. The new string gave a much lower note.
   新弦发出的音低了许多。

3. He stayed silent for a moment, then played the same tune again.
   他安静了一会儿，又弹起了同一段曲调。

4. This time, he used the low note at the end.
   这回，他把那个低音用在了最后。

5. It sounded like someone saying goodnight.
   听起来就像有人在道晚安。
