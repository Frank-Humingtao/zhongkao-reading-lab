### 第 1 段

1. My niece loved a plain green jacket that had once belonged to her brother.
   我外甥女喜欢一件纯绿色的外套，那是她哥哥以前穿过的。

2. By winter, its sleeves were too short, and one pocket had a hole.
   到了冬天，袖子已经太短了，其中一个口袋也破了个洞。

3. She tucked the jacket into a bag and took it to a tailor.
   她把外套塞进袋子里，拿去给裁缝看。

4. “Can you mend it and make the sleeves longer?” she asked.
   “您能补好它，再把袖子做长一点吗？”她问。

5. The tailor gave a nod and took a tape measure from the shelf.
   裁缝点点头，从架子上拿下一把软尺。

### 第 2 段

1. On a table lay two separate piles of cloth, one thick and one thin.
   桌上分开放着两摞布料，一摞厚的，一摞薄的。

2. She added thick yellow cloth to both sleeves, with a neat row of stitches along each edge.
   裁缝给两只袖子都接上厚黄布，沿着每条边工工整整地缝了一排针脚。

3. When we came back, a small yellow star covered the hole in the pocket.
   我们回来时，口袋上的破洞已经被一颗黄色小星星盖住了。

4. The tailor slipped the receipt and the leftover cloth into an envelope.
   裁缝把收据和剩下的布料塞进一个信封。

5. My niece held up her arms.
   外甥女举起双臂。

6. “Look! It grew!”
   “看！它长大了！”
