## 双语正文

### 第 1 段

1. A little book box stood opposite our bus stop.
   公交车站对面有个小书箱。

2. Its plain blue door had lost its handle, so nobody could open it.
   书箱有扇纯蓝色的门，门把手掉了，谁也打不开。

3. I got a piece of string from our kitchen cupboard to mend it until we could fit a proper handle.
   我从厨房柜子里找了段细绳，把门暂时修好，等有空再装个合适的把手。

4. Inside, there was a neat pile of books on the bottom shelf.
   里面最下面的搁板上整整齐齐地放着一摞书。

5. Beneath the pile, I found an envelope with no name on it.
   我在书堆下发现一个没写名字的信封。

### 第 2 段

1. A receipt was tucked inside.
   信封里塞着一张收据。

2. On the back of the receipt, someone had left a note: “Please take a book and leave one for the next reader.”
   有人在收据背面留了张便条：“请拿一本书，再给下一位读者留一本。”

3. I taped the note to the door.
   我用胶带把便条贴到了门上。

4. I left my favourite novel there.
   我把自己最喜欢的小说留在了书箱里。

5. The next day, my favourite novel was gone, and a book about the moon sat in its place.
   第二天，我最喜欢的那本小说被人拿走了，原处多了一本讲月球的书。
