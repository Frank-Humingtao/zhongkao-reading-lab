### 第 1 段

1. Our street was planning a picnic, and I was assigned to invite the people who lived nearby.

   “从这道门进去，免得惊动另一道门旁边的狗。”她说。

3. I followed her map.
   我照着她画的地图走。

4. At the first door, a boy gave me a pear before I could even ask his name.
   在第一户门口，我还没来得及问名字，一个男孩就递给我一个梨。
