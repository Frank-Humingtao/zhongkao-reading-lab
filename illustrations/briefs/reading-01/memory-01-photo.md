### 第 1 段

1. I was assigned to take a photograph for a local exhibition.
   我分到的任务是给本地展览拍一张照片。

2. Every evening at six, a girl by the fence opposite my window pulled on a long piece of string.
   每天傍晚六点，我窗户对面的围栏旁都有个女孩在拉一根长细绳。

3. I could think of no explanation, so one day I went down to ask her.
   我想不出她为什么这样做，于是有一天下楼去问她。

4. She put a finger to her lips.
   她把手指放到嘴唇上。

5. I stayed silent and looked where she was pointing.
   我安静地顺着她指的方向看去。

### 第 2 段

1. A basket rose past the fence and stopped beneath a second-floor window.
   一只篮子越过围栏升了起来，在二楼一扇窗户下停住。

2. An old man at the window pulled the basket in.
   窗边的一位老人把篮子拉了进去。

3. “That's Grandpa's supper,” the girl whispered.
   “那是爷爷的晚饭。”女孩低声说。

4. “The doorbell disturbs the baby downstairs.”
   “门铃会吵到楼下的小宝宝。”

5. I took one photograph and wrote a plain caption: “Waiting for supper.”
   我拍了张照片，配上一句简单的说明：“等晚饭。”

6. When I showed her the picture, she gave me a quick nod.
   我把照片拿给她看，她飞快地点了点头。
