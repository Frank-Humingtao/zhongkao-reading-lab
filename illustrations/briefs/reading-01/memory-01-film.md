### 第 1 段

1. My cousin and I made a film about two astronauts who hear thunder inside their spacecraft.
   我和表亲拍了一部短片，讲的是两名宇航员在航天器里听见雷声的故事。

2. Our script called for a loud bang, but we had no proper sound equipment.
   剧本需要一声巨响，可我们没有合适的录音设备。

3. I pulled a metal tray from the cupboard and shook it beneath a thick cushion.
   我从柜子里取出一个金属托盘，在厚坐垫底下晃了晃。

4. The sound was so soft it wouldn't disturb a sleeping cat.
   声音轻得连睡着的猫都不会惊动。

5. Then the tray slipped out and hit the floor.
   接着托盘滑了出去，砸在地板上。

### 第 2 段

1. We went silent.
   我们一下子安静了。

2. There was the bang we wanted!
   这正是我们想要的那一声巨响！

3. My cousin taped a note in neat letters to the cupboard: “Drop the tray from here.”
   表亲用胶带把一张字迹工整的便条贴在柜子上：“从这个高度放下托盘。”

4. We put a phone on a shelf to record the sound.
   我们把手机放在架子上录音。

5. Later, a classmate asked for an explanation of how we had made the thunder.
   后来，同学问我们这雷声是怎么做出来的。

6. I showed her the tray.
   我把托盘拿给她看。

7. She went home and tried it herself.
   她回家后也自己试了一遍。
