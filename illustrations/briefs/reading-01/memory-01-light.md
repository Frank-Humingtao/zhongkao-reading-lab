### 第 1 段

1. One evening, all the lights in our building went out.
   一天晚上，我们楼里的灯全灭了。

2. I heard a bang in the corridor and found my neighbour sitting on the stairs.
   我听见走廊里砰的一声，发现邻居坐在楼梯上。

3. He had slipped in the dark, and his foot was sore.
   他在黑暗中滑了一跤，脚有些疼。

4. My aunt helped him while I looked in our cupboard.
   姨妈去扶他，我跑去翻家里的柜子。

5. A small lamp and an envelope full of spare batteries were on the top shelf.
   最高一层搁板上有一盏小灯和一个装着备用电池的信封。

### 第 2 段

1. We set it on a shelf opposite the stairs and taped a note to the wall beneath it: “Please leave the lamp on.”
   我们把灯放在楼梯对面的架子上，在灯下的墙上贴了张便条：“请让灯一直亮着。”

2. By morning, two more lamps stood on the shelf.
   到了早上，架子上又多了两盏灯。

3. Only then did I realise that the people upstairs had been in the dark, too.
   我这才意识到，楼上的住户昨晚也摸着黑。

4. One little lamp had helped a lot of people.
   一盏小灯帮到了不少人。
