### 第 1 段

1. At a sports club, our instructor set two bags on the ground to make a goal.
   在体育俱乐部，教练把两个包放在地上，当作球门。

2. He set a ball between them and rolled it towards us.
   他把一个球放在两个包中间，朝我们滚过来。

3. We heard a bell inside it.
   我们听见球里有铃铛声。

4. “This is proper equipment for players who can't see,” he said.
   “这是给看不见的球员用的正规器材。”他说。

5. We stood in two separate lines and took turns closing our eyes and trying to stop the ball.
   我们站成两列，轮流闭上眼睛，试着拦住球。

### 第 2 段

1. I could hear the bell until a loud bang from the opposite end of the hall made me turn my head.
   我本来能听见铃声，直到礼堂另一头传来砰的一声，我转过头去。

2. The ball slipped beneath my hand.
   球从我手底下溜了过去。

3. Then I realised why we had to stay silent.
   这时我明白了为什么大家得保持安静。

4. Even a small sound could disturb a player who was listening for the ball.
   哪怕一点声音，也可能干扰靠听觉判断球在哪儿的队员。

5. On my next turn, I listened and caught it.
   下一轮轮到我时，我仔细听，接住了球。

6. My team waited until the bell stopped before they began to clap.
   我的队友等铃声停了才开始鼓掌。
